6. 📞 VoiceClone Hotline — Accessible Phone Assistant for Elderly Users Family members record their voice, and the app creates a familiar-sounding AI assistant for elderly relatives — so they can ask "their family" simple questions (weather, reminders, medication times) in a voice they trust. Why it's useful: Combats tech anxiety in older adults. Core tech: Voice cloning API (e.g. ElevenLabs), simple NLP, telephony API
